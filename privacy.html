ScanVault — Privacy Policy

True North Interactive · Last updated October 1, 2026

ScanVault is made by True North Interactive. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. Last updated October 1, 2026.

Your collection stays on your device. The cards you scan, your photos, prices, and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your phone. ScanVault has no user accounts, and we do not collect, receive, or sell your personal information.

Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you scan a card or choose a photo. Card text is read on your device, and lookups send that text (for example a card name or number) to public card databases to find the card and its prices. Your photos themselves are not uploaded to us.

Third-party data. Card details and prices come from public sources including TCGdex, the Pokémon TCG API, YGOPRODeck, Scryfall, the One Piece Card Game API, the Lorcana API, and PriceCharting. Those services receive the search terms needed to answer your lookup, the same way visiting their websites would.

Links out. Some buttons open outside websites, such as eBay. As an eBay Partner we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases. Once you leave the app, the other site's privacy policy applies.

No ads and no trackers. ScanVault does not show ads and does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Deleting your data. You can remove cards inside the app at any time. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
